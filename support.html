Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Gomoku?

Gomoku is a two-player strategy game. Players take turns placing stones on the board. The first player to get five stones in a row - horizontally, vertically, or diagonally - wins.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Gomoku works entirely offline. No internet connection is needed to play.

Does the app collect any personal data?

No. Gomoku does not collect, store, or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The app crashed or is not working. What should I do?

Try closing the app fully and reopening it. If the problem continues, please contact us at the email below and describe what happened.
